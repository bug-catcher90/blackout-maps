Fire, Gas and a Safe Home

Blackouts bring flames and batteries indoors. Candles, gas heaters, camping stoves and chargers cause many of the fires and carbon monoxide poisonings that follow an outage, often at night.

The emergency pages are drawn like the safety card on a plane: get out and close the door, crawl under smoke, seal a room if you are trapped, stop, drop and roll, smother a pan fire, use an extinguisher, and what to do about a smell of gas or a CO alarm.

Prepare covers smoke and CO alarms that keep working in a blackout, the right extinguisher and fire blanket, an escape plan for a flat or a house, and safe habits with candles, heaters, gas bottles, electricity and lithium batteries.

  • Smoke and CO alarms: which to buy, where to fit them, how to test them
  • Extinguishers and fire blankets, and how to use them
