Power in a Blackout

The lights go out. In ten minutes you will know who needs help, how to keep one phone alive and why the fridge stays shut. In a weekend, you will know exactly how much backup power your home needs, and what it can run.

Power in a Blackout starts with people and safety, then explains the four words behind every battery and label: volts, amps, watts and watt-hours. You measure what your own home uses, apply one simple rule to size a power bank, a power station or a generator, and plan for one night, three days or a week.

A Lisbon family carries every calculation from the first meter reading to day seven, so you can check each number and swap in your own.

Inside

  • Eight emergency pages for an outage already under way, from the first ten minutes to the moment power returns
  • The three-step sizing rule and the power ladder: what each level of kit really runs, from a power bank to a station with solar
  • Choosing and using power banks, power stations and fuel generators safely, including where never to run one
  • Recharging without the grid: realistic numbers for solar, a car and a generator
  • Five household plans: one adult, a family flat, a house with a well, medical devices, cold and heat
  • Appliance tables, a plug and voltage guide, and printable forms for your own inventory and 7-day battery budget

48 illustrated pages. Written after the Iberian blackout of 28 April 2025.
