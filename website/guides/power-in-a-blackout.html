Power in a Blackout is how a home stays livable when the grid stops. If the outage is already happening, Now is the part in your hand: who is stuck or on a medical device, light that is not a candle, the cooker off, the fridge left shut, and one phone kept for news. Prepare is that same problem learned beforehand, what light, phones and cold food cost in energy, and a rehearsed backup for one night, three days or a week, from a power bank to a station, a generator or a little solar. Reference sits beside the plan as the thing you look up: what ordinary appliances draw, which plugs and voltages you have, and forms for the household’s own numbers. A flat with two adults and two children carries the example, and their figures are there to be replaced with yours. The Iberian blackout of 28 April 2025 is why the book starts with people. A power bank that has been tested is worth more than a large station still in the box.
