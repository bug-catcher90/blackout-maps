Food Without Power is for the afternoon the fridge starts to warm and the shops may not take a card. The fridge is the clock. In the first hour you keep both doors shut, write down the time, switch off the hob so it cannot come back on by itself, and plan today’s meals from what will spoil, not from the cupboard. The order is fridge first, then freezer, then the food you stored. If something has been warm and you are not sure, throw it out. The book is firm that you never taste food to find out whether it is safe.

When you are preparing, it helps you build a pantry of food you already like and can eat cold, then rotate it so nothing sits until it expires. It covers meals with no cooking, cooking on fuel, with retained heat or from a battery, babies and special diets, medicines that need to stay cold, and the one shopping trip you get when the tills are down. During the blackout of 28 April 2025, ready-to-eat food was among the first things to disappear, and the households that coped had a cupboard they could eat from without an oven. The worked example is the Silva family in Lisbon, Ana, Rui, Inês, Tomás and their dog. The plan runs from three days without cooking to a week of simple cooking and, if you want it, two weeks, with shopping lists and a food-safety card at the back.
