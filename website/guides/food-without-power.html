Food Without Power is for a fridge that is warming and for shops that may only take cash. Now is the outage as it happens: the cold kept in, today’s meals taken from what will spoil, the cupboard left for later, and anything warm thrown out rather than tasted. Prepare is the pantry built on ordinary weeks, food you already like and can eat with no cooking, rotated so it does not expire in the dark, and a way of cooking on fuel, retained heat or a battery that has been tried. Reference is what you look up instead of remembering: shopping lists, shelf lives, a food-safety card, and forms for the household’s own stores. A Lisbon family of four and a dog is the worked example. The book runs from a few days with no cooking out to a fortnight for anyone who wants a deeper cupboard. On 28 April 2025, ready-to-eat food was among the first things to disappear.
