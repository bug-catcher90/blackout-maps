Food Without Power

42-page PDF

€7.40

A warming fridge is a clock. This book tells you what to eat first, what to throw away and what to keep for day three, then helps you build a cupboard of food your family will actually eat without cooking.

Clear rules on time and temperature replace guesswork about what is still safe. Food is planned in simple daily baskets of about 2,000 kcal a person, so a 72-hour box for a family of four fits in two crates, and a week needs only one more crate of staples.

Cooking without electricity is covered safely: gas hobs lit with a match, camping stoves outdoors, retained-heat cooking that saves most of the fuel, and what a power station can and cannot cook.

Inside

  • The eating order: fridge, freezer, fresh, then cupboard
  • Food-safety rules for warm fridges, thawed freezers and damaged tins
  • A one-trip shopping list for when an outage is announced and cards may not work
  • The food ladder, shelf lives, and rotation without waste
  • Babies, diabetes, allergies, older people and pets
  • A 7-day menu for a family, and plans for one person and a house in the country
