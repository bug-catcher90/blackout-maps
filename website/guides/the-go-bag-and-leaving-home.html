The Go-Bag and Leaving Home is for knowing whether to stay, and for leaving well if home is the danger. Now is that decision while it is happening: an official order to go, a building that is no longer safe, ten minutes or an hour, by car or on foot, and a way to find your people when the phone map is dead. Prepare is the bag and the plan made beforehand, light enough to carry, with documents, cash and medicines, room for children, older people and pets, routes that need no network, and a grab-and-go drill that has been timed. Reference is what you should not have to invent in a doorway: the packing checklist, the car kit, a card for the door, and wallet cards to print and carry. A Lisbon family and their dog are packed out in full. Underneath the packing is the Pedrógão Grande fire, when leaving late by car was the choice that killed. Staying is usually safer. The book is how you tell which one this is.
