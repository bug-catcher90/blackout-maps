The Go-Bag and Leaving Home

43-page PDF

€7.80

The Go-Bag and Leaving Home is the guide for the decision to leave: when to leave and when to stay, what to grab in ten minutes, how to pack a bag for three days, and how to find your way and your family when phones and maps go dark.

The rule it puts above the rest: if the authorities tell you to leave, leave, and leave early. It says people are rarely hurt by leaving too soon, and are hurt by leaving too late.

Staying at home is usually safest. The book starts with the decision, not the bag. It uses the Pedrógão Grande fire of June 2017, when many of the people who died were caught on a road while trying to escape by car. The same Lisbon family appears, with their 12 kg dog, Bolacha. The lessons: decide in advance what will make you leave; a packed bag makes ten minutes enough; families are often split when trouble starts, so a meeting plan on paper has to work when phones fail.

Part A is go or stay, leaving in ten minutes or an hour, by car or on foot, finding the way offline, where to go, and going home again. Part B is what goes in the bag and how much it should weigh, documents, money, medicines, children, older people, people with disabilities, pets, a car kit, routes that work without phones, and a grab-and-go drill with a timer.
