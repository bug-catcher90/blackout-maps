The Go-Bag and Leaving Home

43-page PDF

€7.80

Most emergencies are safest at home. Some are not. This book helps you tell the difference, and leave well when you have to.

It starts with the decision: a simple stay-or-go chart, the signs that should make you leave early, and why leaving late by car is often the most dangerous choice of all. Then come the ten-minute and one-hour checklists, driving near smoke and flood water, walking with children, and finding your way with an offline map or a paper one.

In Prepare you pack one bag per person, weighed item by item (an adult's comes in under 9 kg), and agree three meeting points and two routes that work without any network.

Inside

  • A stay-or-go chart and a ten-minute card for the inside of your front door
  • The go-bag list in twelve groups, with a real weight budget
  • Documents, cash, medicines, children's bags and pets
  • The car kit and what to carry every day
  • The family meeting plan, routes that avoid bridges and forest roads, and map and compass basics
  • Five plans: a building evacuated at night, leaving on foot, wildfire, reduced mobility, a flood warning

43 illustrated pages, with checklists and wallet cards to print.
