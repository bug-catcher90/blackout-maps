In a blackout, a storm or a flood, the ambulance may be far away. For a while, the first aider is you.

Ten emergency pages, ordered by urgency and drawn step by step, cover the first minute, CPR for adults, children and babies, severe bleeding and tourniquets, choking, the recovery position, chest pain and stroke, burns, anaphylaxis and asthma, and heat and cold. A clear guide tells you when to call 112, when to drive to care, and when it can wait.

It is a companion to a course, not a replacement. Prepare explains how to get trained, what to keep in a home kit and a long-crisis kit, and how to look after wounds, fractures, fever, dehydration and stress for hours rather than minutes.
