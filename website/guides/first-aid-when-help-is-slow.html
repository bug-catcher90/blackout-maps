First Aid When Help Is Slow

47-page PDF

€9.40

First Aid When Help Is Slow is for the time between an injury and professional care, when a blackout, a storm or a flood means the ambulance is not minutes away. Now is that time as it happens, one emergency to a page, from breathing and bleeding through choking, chest pain, burns and heat, and a clear line between calling 112, going, or waiting. It is a reminder, not a course: CPR and serious bleeding are things to have practised with an instructor, and the operator on 112 can coach you while you wait. Prepare is what makes the reminder usable, a real course, a kit and the medicines already in the house, and the care of wounds, fever and dehydration across hours rather than minutes. Reference is what you can hold up under stress, the emergencies on cards, the words to give 112, and a health form for each person. The households are a family in Lisbon, a neighbour who lives alone, and a couple in the Alentejo.
