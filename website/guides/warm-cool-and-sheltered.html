Warm, Cool and Sheltered

Heat and cold kill quietly, usually at home. The good news: the body is far easier to protect than the house.

When the heating stops, you choose one room, close it off and warm the people, not the building. In a heatwave you keep the sun off the windows by day, let the night air in, and cool the skin with water. The book shows how in pictures, and gives clear temperatures at which to act, or to move someone vulnerable somewhere safer.

Prepare explains how homes and bodies lose heat, how to set up a warm room and a cool room before the season, which clothes and bedding work, which heaters and fans are safe on a battery, and how to make quick repairs after a storm and shelter from wildfire smoke.
