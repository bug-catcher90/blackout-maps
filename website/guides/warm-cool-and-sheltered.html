Warm, Cool and Sheltered is for a cold house or a hot one when the power that usually fixes it is gone. Heatwaves and cold homes in southern Europe kill more people than most storms, usually at home, and often before anyone notices. Now is that weather already in the rooms: one space closed off, the people warmed or cooled rather than the whole building, and a move somewhere else if a baby, an older person or someone ill cannot be kept safe. Prepare is that room set up before the season, how the house gains and loses heat, clothes and bedding that work without electricity, heaters and fans that are safe on a battery, and a repair rehearsed for a broken window or smoke outside. Reference is the card you trust over a guess: safe temperatures, checklists for the warm room and the cool room, and a log of what those rooms actually did. The temperatures are the World Health Organization’s, at least 18 °C in cold weather, and heat treated as a risk above about 32 °C by day or 26 °C at night. The body is easier to protect than the house.
