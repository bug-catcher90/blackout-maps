Water When the Tap Stops

43-page PDF

€7.80

Water When the Tap Stops is for drinking, cooking and washing when the tap has stopped, or when the water is running and still not safe. Now is that hour: whatever pressure is left goes into drinking containers first, flushing and washing stay a separate stock, and anything uncertain is treated before it meets teeth, food or a baby’s bottle. Prepare is the skill learned while the tap still works, how much the household needs, what boiling, chlorine and a filter each do, and a labelled stock that has been rotated and rehearsed. Reference is the sheet next to the pot: treatment doses, how much each job uses, and forms to print and keep with the containers. The example is a fourth-floor flat in Lisbon, planned from the European Union’s 72 hours, about 12 litres a person, and shown stretched toward a week. On 28 April 2025 the upper floors went dry when the pumps stopped. The homes that had filled bottles and a bath in the first hour hardly noticed.
