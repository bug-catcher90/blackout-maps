Water When the Tap Stops

43-page PDF

€7.80

Most homes hold more water than they think: in the pipes, the water heater, the cistern. This book shows you how to find it, make it safe, and make it last a week.

The first ten minutes decide a lot: fill bottles and the bath while pressure lasts, switch off the heater, label what is not for drinking. Then the book explains the four kinds of trouble that can be in water and which method deals with each (boiling, bleach, filters, sunlight), with exact doses for the bleach sold in Portuguese and Spanish shops.

You work out your household's daily need, build a stock that fits in a cupboard, and rotate it twice a year when the clocks change.

Inside

  • The first ten minutes, the water hiding in your home, and how to drain a water heater safely
  • A one-page treatment card: boiling, bleach drops by strength, tablets, filters
  • The table that shows which method stops bacteria, viruses, parasites and chemicals
  • Containers, storage and a rotation routine that does not fail
  • Toilets, hygiene, babies, pets and heat without running water
  • Five household plans, from one adult to a house with a well, plus what to do under a boil-water notice

43 illustrated pages, with a cut-out dose card and printable forms.
