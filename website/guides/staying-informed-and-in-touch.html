Staying Informed and in Touch is for the hours when you cannot reach your children, your partner or the news. Now is the network already down: a battery radio on, one phone saved, a short message home, a way to call for help, and a check before a rumour gets passed on. Prepare is the arrangement made while everything still works, the stations the authorities use, a radio and walkie-talkies that have been tried, a phone set up to last, and a family plan of meeting points and check-in times that needs no signal. Reference is that plan on paper: local stations and frequencies, a phone book that is not inside the phone, and message forms to copy. On 28 April 2025 the routers died at once and the public broadcasters stayed on the air. A wind-up radio, a charged power bank and one written sheet are enough to begin, because none of them needs an account.
