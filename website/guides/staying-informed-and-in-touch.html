Staying Informed and in Touch

44-page PDF

€8.20

On 28 April 2025, home internet died at once, mobile networks faded over a few hours, and public radio stayed on air. The families who had agreed a plan found each other without a signal.

This book explains which networks fail first and why, then shows what still works: a battery radio, one phone set up to last, short text messages, walkie-talkies, and a family plan with meeting points and check-in times that needs no network at all.

It also teaches you to check what you hear before you act on it or pass it on, because rumours and scams travel fastest when people are frightened.

Inside

  • The first hour, getting news, reaching your family, and calling 112 on a weak network
  • What fails and when: internet, data, calls, texts and radio on one timeline
  • Radios, official warnings, phone settings and a second network
  • PMR446 walkie-talkies: the rules, real range and how to talk
  • The family plan, messages that work, and a notice board for the building
  • Five situations, from a family spread across the city to being abroad when it happens

44 illustrated pages, with a paper phone book, message templates and a walkie-talkie card.
