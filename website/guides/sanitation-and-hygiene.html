Sanitation and Hygiene

When water, sewers and bin collections stop, illness spreads through hands, food and flies. A bucket, strong bags, soap and plain bleach prevent almost all of it.

The book starts with the decision that matters most: can you still flush into the sewer, or must you keep waste out of it? Then it shows step by step how to flush with a bucket, build a bagged toilet, set up a handwashing station, store rubbish safely, and clean up after vomiting or flood water.

Prepare explains how germs spread, how much water hygiene really needs, the bleach dilution for each job, and how to manage periods, nappies, laundry and pests for a week without running water.

Inside

  • A toilet without water and a toilet without sewers, illustrated
  • Handwashing with half a litre, and a station you can build in minutes
  • Bleach dilutions for everyday surfaces, illness and dishes
  • Diarrhoea and vomiting at home, and cleaning up after a flood
  • Periods, babies, older people, laundry, rubbish and pests
  • Four situations, from three days without water to a septic pump down after a storm

42 illustrated pages, with a bleach card, toilet and handwashing cards for the bathroom door, and a sick-room plan.
